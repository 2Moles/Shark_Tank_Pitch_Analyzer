## 📊 Output Format

Users upload a pitch audio file (30 seconds - 10 minutes) and receive:

1. **Overall Score** (0-10): Combined vocal + content
2. **Verdict**: Invest / Need More Info / Not Invest
3. **Vocal Delivery Breakdown**:
   - Clarity: 0-10
   - Energy: 0-10
   - Confidence: 0-10
4. **Business Content Breakdown**:
   - Problem: 0-10
   - Solution: 0-10
   - Market: 0-10
   - Revenue Model: 0-10
   - Competition: 0-10
5. **Transcription**: Full text with metrics
6. **Shark Feedback**: 5 personas' perspectives
7. **Consensus Verdict**: Panel recommendation
8. **Audio Analysis**: Detailed vocal metrics
9. **Emotional Analysis**: Audio + text emotions
